Privacy by design隐私优先设计

Your mail stays between you and the services you choose.

CF Mail is built as a client. Standard mail traffic goes to your configured provider; CloudMail traffic goes to the CloudMail Server you configure.

你的邮件，只在你与所选服务之间流转。

CF Mail 的定位是邮件客户端。标准邮箱流量发送到你配置的服务商；CloudMail 流量发送到你配置的 CloudMail Server。

🔒

No ad network. No tracking SDK.

无广告网络，无跟踪 SDK。

The current iOS project uses MailCore2 for mail protocol support and does not integrate third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

当前 iOS 项目使用 MailCore2 提供邮件协议能力，不集成第三方广告或行为分析 SDK。

FAQ常见问题

Straight answers.

常见问题。

Does CF Mail run its own mail cloud?CF Mail 是否运营自己的邮件云服务？

CF Mail is primarily a client. Standard IMAP/SMTP accounts connect to the provider you configure. CloudMail accounts connect to the CloudMail Server you configure.

CF Mail 主要作为客户端运行。标准 IMAP/SMTP 账户连接你配置的邮箱服务商；CloudMail 账户连接你配置的 CloudMail Server。

Where are passwords stored?密码保存在哪里？

Account credentials and tokens are stored using iOS Keychain where applicable.

账户密码和 token 在适用情况下使用 iOS 钥匙串保存。

Does CF Mail upload diagnostics?CF Mail 会自动上传诊断数据吗？

No automatic diagnostic upload is built into the current app. Logs stay local by default and are redacted before a user-initiated export.

当前 App 不会自动上传诊断数据。日志默认保存在本地，并会在用户主动导出前进行脱敏。

Can I clear local data?可以清除本地数据吗？

Yes. CF Mail includes controls for cache, diagnostics, account removal, and Reset All Data. Server-side messages are not deleted by a local reset.

可以。CF Mail 提供缓存、诊断、账户移除和“重置所有数据”等控制；本地重置不会删除服务器上的邮件。
